## Get the app and create your account

> The **App Store** and **Google Play** buttons on the website are placeholders
> until the app is published. Until then the club gives testers the app
> directly.

1. Open the Queenix app.
2. On **Welcome back**, tap **Sign up** (at the bottom, after "Don't have an
   account?").
3. On **Create your account**, fill in:
   - **Full name**
   - **Email**
   - **Password** (at least 8 characters)
   - **Confirm password**
4. Tap **Create account**.

You are signed in straight away and land on the **Home** tab. The app may first
show a short 3-step setup ("A bit about you", "Health declaration",
"Your goals"). Those answers are **not saved yet**, so please also fill in
[Health & preferences](#health-details-vehicles-and-id) afterwards.

**Sign-up messages**

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Please complete all fields* | A box is empty |
| *Password must be at least 8 characters* | Choose a longer password |
| *Passwords do not match* | The two password boxes are different |
| *User already exists* (or similar) | That email already has an account. Tap **Sign in** and use **Forgot password?** if needed |

## Verify your email

When you sign up, we email you a 6-digit code. The subject line reads
"*123456* is your Queenix Gym code".

1. On the **Home** tab, tap the **Verify your email** card.
   (You can also go to **Profile** → **Privacy & security**.)
2. In the **Email** card, type the code in **Verification code**.
3. Tap **Verify**. You should see *"Email verified"* and the badge changes from
   **Not verified** to **Verified**.

Didn't get the code? Wait a minute, check your spam or junk folder, then tap
**Resend code**. Codes expire after 10 minutes, and you get 5 tries per code.

## Signing in

The sign-in screen has two buttons at the top: **Email** and **Phone**.

**With email**

1. Tap **Email**.
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Tap **Sign in**.

**With a text message (SMS)**: only after you have added your phone (see
[Security settings](#security-settings)).

1. Tap **Phone**.
2. Type your **Phone number** with the country code, for example
   `+971501234567`.
3. Tap **Text me a code**.
4. Type the 6-digit **SMS code** and tap **Verify & sign in**.
   Wrong number? Tap **Use a different number**.

**Forgot your password?**

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?**
2. Type your **Email** and tap **Email me a code**.
3. Type the **Code** from the email and a **New password** (8+ characters).
4. Tap **Set new password**, then **Back to sign in**.

## A tour of the app

The bar at the bottom has five tabs.

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your membership, quick actions, how busy the gym is, your next class, points |
| **Book** | Classes to book, and personal trainers |
| **Gym** | Your QR entry code and recent visits |
| **Rewards** | Points, rewards to redeem, referrals |
| **Profile** | Payments, documents, health details, vehicles, ID, notifications, security, help, sign out |

### Home

- The membership card at the top: your plan, days remaining and status. If you
  have no plan it says **No active membership**. Buttons: **View membership**
  (opens **Profile**) and **Renew** (opens **Payments**).
- **Quick actions**: **My QR** (Gym tab), **Book class** (Book tab),
  **Trainer** (list of trainers).
- **Live occupancy**: how many people are in the gym right now.
- **Up next**: your next class. Tap **See all** to open the Book tab.
- **This month**: your **Points**, **Upcoming** classes and **In-gym** count.
- **Refer a friend**: opens the Rewards tab.

### Profile

| Item | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Payments & membership** | Your plan, cards, receipts, pause/cancel |
| **Documents** | Agreements and waivers to sign |
| **Health & preferences** | Date of birth, gender, emergency contact |
| **Vehicles & parking** | Car plates the valet desk recognises |
| **Verify your ID** card | Scan your Emirates ID or passport |
| **Preferences** card | **Push notifications** and **Marketing emails** switches; language (English, Arabic soon) |
| **Notifications** | Messages from the club |
| **Privacy & security** | Verify email, add phone, change password |
| **Help & support** | Send a request to the team |
| **Sign out** | Signs you out of the app |

## Buy or change a plan

1. Go to **Profile** → **Payments & membership**.
2. If you have no plan, tap **View plans**. If you have one, tap
   **Change plan**.
3. On **Choose a plan**, read each plan (price, length in days, features).
4. Tap **Choose** followed by the plan name (for example **Choose Premium**).
5. Finish the payment:
   - If the club uses **Tap**, your browser opens the payment page. The app
     says *"Complete payment in your browser"*.
   - If the club uses **Stripe**, the app shows a test-card message and opens
     your receipt page.

> **Test mode:** card payments currently run in test mode. No real money is
> taken. Your plan becomes active when the payment provider confirms the
> payment. If it doesn't, ask the front desk; they can take payment at the
> counter.

Questions about plans: **hello@queenixgym.com** (shown at the bottom of the
plans screen).

## Book a class

1. Tap the **Book** tab. The **Classes** button at the top is selected.
2. Optional: filter by **All**, **HIIT**, **Yoga**, **Strength**, **Cardio** or
   **Pilates**.
3. Each class card shows the time, room, trainer and how full it is:
   **Spots open**, **Almost full** or **Full**.
4. Tap **Book class**. A message says "Booked" with the class name, and the
   class page opens.

**Class is full?** Tap **Join waitlist** on the card. It opens the class page;
tap **Join waitlist** there. You'll see *"On the waitlist — we will notify you
of a spot"*.

**On the class page** (tap any class card) the big button at the bottom
changes:

| Button | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Book this class** | Spots are open | Books you in |
| **Join waitlist (N waiting)** | Class is full | Adds you to the waitlist |
| **Cancel booking** | You are booked | Cancels your place |
| **Leave waitlist** | You are on the waitlist | Removes you from the waitlist |

Your booked classes show **Booked ✓** on the Book tab, and waitlisted ones show
**On waitlist**.

## Personal training

- **Book** tab → **Personal Training** shows the trainers, their specialty and
  price ("From AED … per hour").
- **Home** → **Trainer** opens **Trainers**, with filters (**All**, **Yoga**,
  **HIIT**, **Strength**, **Cardio**, **Pilates**).

You **cannot book a personal training session yourself** in the app yet. The
**Book** buttons on trainer cards only move between these lists, and every
trainer shows "Contact for availability". Ask your trainer or the front desk;
your trainer schedules the session for you and it then appears in your
schedule.

## Enter the gym with your QR code

1. Tap the **Gym** tab (or **My QR** on Home). The screen is called
   **Gym access**.
2. Show the QR code to the scanner or the front desk.

- The code changes every 60 seconds for security. The label shows
  "Refreshes in …s". Tap **Refresh** for a new code at any time.
- If you see **QR unavailable**, tap **Try again**. Check you have internet.
- **Recent visits** lists your last entries (**Entered** / **Exited**), each
  marked **OK** or **Denied** with the reason.

## Payments and receipts

Go to **Profile** → **Payments & membership**. The screen is called
**Payments**.

| Section | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Current plan card | **Change plan**; **Pause** (freezes your plan for 30 days); **Resume** (ends a pause) |
| **This month** / **Member since** | What you paid this month and how long you've been a member |
| **Payment methods** | **Add card**, choose a default card, remove a card |
| **Transaction history** | Every payment. Tap one to open its receipt in your browser |
| **Plan management** | **Cancel membership** |

**Add a card on file**

1. Tap **Add card**.
2. Pick the **Card brand** (visa, mastercard, amex or Apple Pay).
3. Type the **Last 4 digits** and the **Expiry (MM/YY)**. Only the last 4
   digits are stored; never type your full card number.
4. Tap **Save card**. You'll see *"Card saved on file"*.

**Cancel your membership**

> Tapping **Cancel membership** cancels straight away; there is no "are you
> sure?" step. You keep access until the end date shown on the card
> ("You will keep access until …").

Questions about billing: **hello@queenixgym.com**.

## Rewards and referrals

Tap the **Rewards** tab.

- **Your points** card: your balance and tier. **How to earn** shows how you
  earn points ("100 pts per class, 500 for referrals"). **Tiers** opens
  **Tier breakdown**, which lists each tier and its perks.
- **Refer a friend** card ("Earn 500 points when they join. They get 200."):
  tap it to create your referral code. A message shows your code.
  Write the code down or take a screenshot: the app says the link was
  copied, but it is **not** copied to your clipboard yet.
- Three buttons switch the list below:
  - **Redeem**: rewards and their cost in points. Tap **Redeem**, then show
    the confirmation at the front desk.
  - **History**: points earned and spent.
  - **Referrals**: people who used your code and their status.

## Documents

1. Go to **Profile** → **Documents**.
2. The top card shows how many you've signed (for example "3/5 signed").
3. Documents are grouped under **Action required**, **Signed** and
   **Optional**.
4. Tap a document to read it.
5. To sign, type your full legal name in the box (it says
   **Full legal name**) and tap **Read & sign**. Your typed name is your
   signature. A message confirms it was signed.

Signed documents show **Signed by**, **Signed on** and **Version**. If you
signed on paper at the desk, it shows "Paper copy (scanned at the desk)".

## Health details, vehicles and ID

### Health & preferences

**Profile** → **Health & preferences** ("Used by trainers in an emergency").

1. Under **About you**: **Date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD)** and **Gender**.
2. Under **Emergency contact**: **Full name**, **Phone**,
   **Relationship (optional)**. Name and phone are required.
3. Tap **Save health details**.

### Vehicles & parking

**Profile** → **Vehicles & parking**. The valet desk uses these plates to find
you.

1. Under **Add vehicle**, type the **Plate number**, and optionally
   **Make (optional)**, **Model (optional)** and **Color (optional)**.
2. Tap **Add vehicle**.
3. To remove one, tap **Remove** on its card.

### Verify your ID

On **Profile**, the **Verify your ID** card:

1. Choose **Emirates ID** or **Passport**.
2. Tap **Take photo** (or **From library**).
3. Check the details the app read (document number, name, nationality, dates)
   and correct any mistakes.
4. Tap **Confirm & save**, or **Discard** to start again.

Your ID then shows as "self-scanned, staff will confirm at the desk".

## Notifications

**In-app inbox:** **Profile** → **Notifications**.

- The top shows how many are unread, or "All caught up".
- New messages have a **New** badge. Tap a message to mark it read.
- **Mark all read** clears them all.

**Phone alerts (push):** when you first sign in, your phone asks to allow
notifications. Say **Allow** to get club announcements on your lock screen.
Tapping an alert opens the Notifications screen.

- Push only works in the published app, not in test versions.
- The **Push notifications** switch in **Profile** → **Preferences** saves your
  preference, but to stop phone alerts completely, also turn off notifications
  for Queenix in your phone's settings.
- **Marketing emails** controls offers and news by email.

## Security settings

**Profile** → **Privacy & security**.

| Card | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Email** | Shows **Verified** or **Not verified**. Enter a **Verification code** and tap **Verify**, or tap **Resend code** |
| **Phone** | Add a phone to sign in by SMS (steps below) |
| **Change password** | Type **Current password**, **New password (8+ characters)**, **Confirm new password**, then tap **Change password** |
| **Your data** | Who can see your details. To delete your account, contact the front desk |

**Add your phone for SMS sign-in**

1. In the **Phone** card, type your **Phone number** with the country code
   (for example `+971501234567`).
2. Tap **Text me a code**.
3. Type the 6-digit **SMS code** and tap **Confirm phone**.
4. You'll see *"Phone verified — you can now sign in by SMS"*.

Changing your password signs you out on all your other devices.

## Help and support

**Profile** → **Help & support** ("We reply within a day").

1. Under **New request**, type a **Subject** and **What happened?**
2. Choose a **Category**: general, billing, access or class.
3. Tap **File request**. You'll see *"Ticket filed — the team will respond"*.

Your requests are listed under **My requests** with their status (open,
in progress, waiting, resolved).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *"Invalid email or password"* | Wrong details | Check spelling or use **Forgot password?** |
| *"Too many requests. Please try again later."* | Too many sign-in attempts in a minute | Wait one minute |
| *"No account uses this number yet…"* | That phone isn't linked to an account | Sign in with email, then add your phone in **Privacy & security** |
| *"Enter your number with country code…"* | Phone number missing the `+971` part | Type it like `+971501234567` |
| *"Invalid OTP"*, *"OTP expired"* or *"Too many attempts"* | The code is wrong, older than 10 minutes, or used 5 times | Request a new code |
| Verification code didn't arrive | Email delay or spam filter | Wait a minute, check spam/junk, then tap **Resend code** |
| **QR unavailable** on the Gym tab | No connection or a temporary error | Tap **Try again**; check your internet |
| Scanner says **Denied** ("Token expired" or "Invalid token") | The code was old or mistyped | Tap **Refresh** and show the new code |
| *"Could not book this class"* | The class may be full, cancelled or already started | Pull the list again or try another class |
| Plan still shows **No active membership** after paying | Test-mode payment not confirmed | Ask the front desk |
| Tried to sign in on the website and got sent back | Member accounts work only in the app | Use the Queenix app |
