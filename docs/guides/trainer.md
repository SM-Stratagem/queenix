# Trainer guide

**Who this is for:** personal trainers and class coaches.

**What this role is for:** running your personal training (PT) clients and
sessions, keeping your profile and certifications up to date, tracking your
earnings and asking for time off. You work mostly in the **Queenix app**; the
staff console has a smaller **Trainer** page.

---

## Signing in and where you land

| Where | How | You land on |
|---|---|---|
| Queenix app | Sign in with your email and password (or by SMS, see [member.md](member.md#signing-in)) | **Today** tab |
| Staff console | Go to `/login` (or **Staff login** on the website) | **Trainer** page |

If you are also a member, see [Switching role](#switching-role).

## Tour of the app

The bar at the bottom has five tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Today's PT sessions, quick stats, availability switch, day-off request |
| **Schedule** | Your week: PT sessions and classes |
| **Clients** | Your PT clients ("My roster"), and **New session** |
| **Earnings** | This month's earnings, pending and paid amounts, early payout |
| **Profile** | Bio, rate, specialties, certifications, documents, sign out |

Screens you reach from those tabs:

| Screen | How to get there |
|---|---|
| **Time off** | **Today** → **Request day off**, or **Schedule** → **Request day off** |
| **New session** | **Clients** → **New session** |
| **Client** | Tap a client on **Clients**, or the notes icon on a session |
| **Documents** | **Profile** → **Documents** |

## Daily tasks

### 1. Check your day (Today tab)

The top card ("Your training day") shows **Sessions**, **Hours booked** and
**Unique members** for today.

Under **Today's schedule**, each PT session shows the time, member, and a
status badge: **Upcoming**, **In progress** or **Completed**. Tap **View week**
to open the Schedule tab.

### 2. Mark a session as done

> **Important:** the **Start** button on an upcoming session marks the session
> **completed** straight away. Tapping the session card itself does the same.
> Only tap **Start** when the session has actually happened.

1. On **Today**, find the session.
2. When it's finished, tap **Start** (or **Resume** if it is in progress).
   A message confirms "Completed PT with" the member's name.
3. To cancel instead, tap **Cancel**. You'll see *"Session cancelled"*.

Note: a session whose time has passed shows **Completed** even if you didn't
tap anything, and a cancelled session also shows as **Completed** (greyed).
Check the client's **Sessions** tab if you need the real status.

### 3. Schedule a new PT session

1. Go to **Clients** and tap **New session**.
2. Under **Member**, type in **Search members by name or email** and tap the
   right person. (Tap **Change** to pick someone else.)
3. Under **When**, type **Date (YYYY-MM-DD)** and **Time (HH:MM)** in 24-hour
   format, for example `2026-11-03` and `18:00`.
4. Under **Details**, set **Duration (min)** and **Price (AED)**, and add
   **Notes (optional)**.
5. Tap **Schedule session**. You'll see *"Session scheduled"*.

The member becomes your client and the session appears on **Today** and
**Schedule**.

### 4. Work with your clients

On **Clients**:

- Type in **Search by name**.
- Filter with **All**, **Active**, **New** or **Attention** (each shows a
  count). **Needs attention** means no session for more than two weeks.
- Each client shows the number of sessions, **Last** and **Next** session.

Tap a client to open **Client**:

- Stats: **Total sessions**, **Completed**, **Last seen**, and an
  **Engagement** bar.
- Three buttons: **Overview** (contact details, profile, goals),
  **Sessions** (session history) and **Notes**.
- To add a coaching note: tap **Notes**, type in **New coaching note…**, tap
  **Save note**. You'll see *"Note saved"*. Finance and admin can read these
  notes on the member's record in their app.

The **Book session** and **Message** buttons on the client card are not working
yet (they only show "Opening…"). Use **New session** on the Clients tab to book.

### 5. Your week (Schedule tab)

- The header shows **This week**. The arrows move between weeks, but the
  sessions shown are always **this week's** for now.
- **PT**, **Classes** and **Hours** count your week.
- **Week** / **Day** switches the view. Colours: **PT**, **Class**, **Free**,
  **Blocked**.
- Tap a PT slot to open that client.
- Tap the calendar icon at the top right (**Share week**) to send your week as
  a message.

### 6. Set yourself available or unavailable

Members see only available trainers.

- **Today** → the first quick action shows **Available** or **Unavailable**.
  Tap it to switch. You'll see *"Marked available"* / *"Marked unavailable"*.
- Or on **Schedule**, tap the big button
  (**Available — tap to go unavailable** / **Unavailable — tap to go available**).

### 7. Ask for a day off

1. Tap **Request day off** (on **Today** or **Schedule**). The screen is called
   **Time off**.
2. Under **Request a day off**, type the **Date (YYYY-MM-DD)**.
3. Choose **Sick day** or **Leave**.
4. Add a **Note (optional)**.
5. Tap **File request**. You'll see *"Day-off request filed"*.

Your requests appear under **My requests** with the status **pending**,
**approved** or **denied**. Operations or finance decide them in the staff
console (**Team & roles** → **Timings & time off**).

### 8. Check your earnings and ask for an early payout

On **Earnings** (this month):

- The top card shows this month's total, the change versus last month and
  **Progress to target**.
- **Pending payout**, **Paid lifetime** and **Total sessions**.
- **Recent transactions** lists each PT session earning with its status
  (**pending**, **paid**, **cancelled**).

To ask to be paid early:

1. Tap **Request early payout**. (It is greyed out when nothing is pending.)
2. You'll see *"Early payout requested — owner notified"*. Finance sees your
   request in **Approvals** (filter **Payout**) in the staff console.

Tap **Share monthly statement** to send this month's list as a message.

### 9. Keep your profile and certifications up to date

On **Profile**:

- **Edit profile** opens a sheet: **Bio**, **Hourly rate (AED)**, and
  **Specialties** (tap to select, for example Strength training,
  Pre/postnatal, Fat loss, Mobility, Hypertrophy). Tap **Save changes**.
- **Edit specialties** (in the Specialties section) opens the same sheet.
- **Add certification** opens a sheet: certification name (for example
  "NASM CPT") and **Issuing body**. Tap **Add certification**.

**Documents** (with expiry dates):

1. **Profile** → **Documents**. The screen lists your **Certifications** with a
   badge: **Valid**, **Expiring soon** (within 60 days), **Expired** or
   **No expiry**.
2. Under **Add document**, fill **Certificate name**, **Issuer**,
   **Issued (YYYY-MM-DD)**, **Expires (optional)** and
   **Document URL (optional)**.
3. Tap **Add document**.

You can also add a certification in the staff console: **Trainer** page →
**My certifications** → **Certification name**, **Issuer** →
**Add certification**.

Other Profile items:

| Item | What it does today |
|---|---|
| **Notifications** switch | Not saved yet |
| **Availability** | Opens the Schedule tab |
| **Language & region** | English only; Arabic is coming |
| **Auto-accept bookings** switch | Not active yet; members can't book you directly in the app |
| **Sign out** | Signs you out |

### 10. Class rosters (staff console)

**Classes** → **Classes overview**:

- **Today's roster**: each class with time, trainer, "booked/capacity" and the
  names of booked members. **Export CSV** downloads the list.
- **Upcoming classes**: the next classes with category, level, length and
  trainer.

## Switching role

If you hold more than one role (for example trainer and member), the app opens
your **active role**. The trainer **Profile** tab has no role switcher yet, so
you can't jump to your member view from there. Ask a superadmin to set your
**Active role** if you need the member view on your phone (see
[README](README.md#giving-someone-a-role)).

---

## What you can't do

| You want to… | Who can help |
|---|---|
| Create or change classes on the timetable | There is no screen for this yet. Ask operations or your developer |
| Approve your own time off | Operations or finance (**Team & roles** → **Timings & time off**) |
| Freeze, cancel or refund a member's plan | Finance or admin |
| Open the member directory in the console | Operations, finance or admin |
| Send notifications or promos (**Notifications**, **Sales push**) | Operations (they can also send phone push) |
| Change your payout or commission rate | Finance |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Notifications** or **Sales push** says *"Live data unavailable — you may not have access…"* | These pages are in your menu but don't load for the trainer role yet | Ask operations to send the message |
| **Staff only** on a page | That page isn't part of the trainer role | Click **Go to my home** |
| A session shows **Completed** that didn't happen | Past sessions are shown as completed automatically | Tell finance so the earning isn't paid |
| *"Pick a member first."* on New session | No member chosen | Search and tap a member |
| *"Use YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM (24h)."* | Date or time typed in the wrong format | Type like `2026-11-03` and `18:00` |
| *"Date must be YYYY-MM-DD"* on Time off | Wrong date format | Type like `2026-11-03` |
| **Request early payout** is greyed out | Nothing is pending | Nothing to request yet |
| Sign-in problems, codes not arriving | See [README](README.md#common-problems-all-roles) | |
